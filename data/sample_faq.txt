To apply for a personal loan you need an identity proof, an address proof and your last three months of salary slips.
The processing fee is charged once, at the time the loan is sanctioned.
You can prepay the loan after six months. A prepayment charge may apply depending on the lender.
If an EMI payment is missed, a late fee is added and your credit score can be affected.
The minimum age to apply for a personal loan is 21 years.
The loan tenure can range from 12 to 60 months.
Interest is calculated on the reducing balance of the loan.
A co-applicant can be added to improve the chances of approval.
Loan approval usually takes two to five working days after documents are verified.
You can check your loan statement anytime in the mobile app.
A credit score above 750 improves your chances of getting a lower interest rate.
The EMI is deducted automatically from your bank account on a fixed date each month.
You can change your EMI date once during the loan tenure by sending a request.
A foreclosure letter is issued within seven days after you close the loan.
Self-employed applicants must submit two years of income tax returns.
The loan amount depends on your monthly income and existing obligations.
